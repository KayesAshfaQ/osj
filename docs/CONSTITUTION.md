# OSJ engineering principles

- Preserve retrieval provenance and reject unsupported citations; do not fabricate law.
- Bundled examples are fictional engineering fixtures and must be labeled clearly.
- Legal information is not legal representation; the demo is not a legal service.
- Treat prompts/context as untrusted. Regex safeguards and privacy redaction are limited.
- Keep credentials and real user/client information outside version control and public traces.
- Keep domain objects separate from framework adapters and test existing HTTP contracts.
- Use TDD for behavior changes and truthful verification records for every change.
- Default to reader-owned local resources. Deployment and provider usage require explicit setup.
