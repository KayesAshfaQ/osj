# Data provenance

`backend/data/examples/demo-acts.json` and `backend/evals/golden_set.jsonl` are
newly authored synthetic OSJ engineering examples, licensed under MIT. The single
fictional Synthetic Demonstration Act has three sections about sample widgets;
the six questions exercise English, Bengali, Banglish, comparison and refusal.
These are not statutes, customer conversations, real case records or legal advice.

No original statutory corpus, historical benchmark or production index is bundled.
Reader-supplied corpora remain local and must have their own verified publication
permissions, source/version metadata and privacy review. Do not assume public
availability grants redistribution rights. Keep downloaded corpora, indexes,
provider logs, evaluation outputs and credentials outside version control.

Synthetic metrics show engineering behavior only. They cannot substantiate legal
accuracy, real-corpus retrieval quality or privacy guarantees. See
[the evaluation guide](../backend/evals/README.md).
