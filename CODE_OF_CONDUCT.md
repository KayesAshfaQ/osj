# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect. Give specific technical feedback, credit other
people's work, welcome questions and protect personal information. Harassment,
threats, discriminatory abuse and publication of others' private information are
not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, close discussions or restrict access
proportionately. Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers using
a private contact channel listed on their public profile; do not post private
incident details or other people's identities in public issues.
